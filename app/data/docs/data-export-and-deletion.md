# Data Export and Account Deletion

You can export all of your notes at any time from Settings, Data, Export. Exports are delivered as a ZIP file containing Markdown files and attachments.

Deleting your account is permanent. After you confirm deletion, your notes are kept in a recoverable state for 14 days and then permanently erased.

Backups that contain your data are purged within 30 days of account deletion.

Team workspace owners must transfer ownership or delete the workspace before deleting their own account.
