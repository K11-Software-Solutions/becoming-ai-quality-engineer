# Pricing Plans

NimbusNotes has three plans: Free, Pro, and Team.

The Free plan includes up to 3 notebooks, 100 MB of storage, and sync on 2 devices.

The Pro plan costs 8 US dollars per month or 80 US dollars per year. Pro includes unlimited notebooks, 20 GB of storage, and sync on unlimited devices.

The Team plan costs 12 US dollars per user per month and requires a minimum of 3 users. Team adds shared workspaces, admin controls, and single sign-on (SSO).

Students with a valid school email address receive 50 percent off the Pro plan.
