# Refund Policy

NimbusNotes offers a full refund within 30 days of the first payment on any paid plan.

Refunds requested after 30 days are not available for monthly plans. Annual plans can receive a prorated refund for unused full months when cancelled after the first 30 days.

To request a refund, open Settings, choose Billing, and select Request refund. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play must be refunded through that store, not through NimbusNotes support.
